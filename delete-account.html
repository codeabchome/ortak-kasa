Account & Data Deletion

How to delete your Ortak Kasa account and data

Delete from the app (recommended)

  1. Open Ortak Kasa and tap the profile icon at the top right.
  2. Tap Delete my account at the bottom.
  3. Your balance must be zero in every group; otherwise the app shows which groups are still open.
  4. Confirm, then verify your identity with your Google account or password.

What is deleted

  • Your login account (Firebase Authentication)
  • Your profile: name, photo URL and IBAN
  • Your device notification tokens
  • Your pending join requests

What stays in groups

So that other members' balances stay correct, expenses and payments you entered remain in the group and are shown as "Deleted user". Your name as it appeared in the activity history and dispute notes may remain visible to that group's members until the group is deleted. Groups you created are archived.

When a group's founder deletes the group, all of its data is permanently deleted after 15 days.

Request by email

If you can't access the app, or you also want your name removed from the remaining records above, email codeabec@gmail.com from the address you used for your account with the subject "Account deletion". Requests are completed within 30 days.
